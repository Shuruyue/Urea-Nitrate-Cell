## Calibration and held-out metrics

The rows marked *metric* compare the model with LSV curves digitized from Q. Yao et al., *Sustainability* **17**, 3835 (2025), CC BY 4.0: the anode curves of Fig. 11a and the cathode curves of Fig. 3b, read from the journal's figure files every 10 mV, with a reading error of about ±1 mA cm⁻² and ±1 mV (`urea_nitrate_cell/yao2025.py`). The S1 parameters are fitted to these same curves, so the calibration rows show how well the fit reproduces its own data; they are not an independent test and carry no threshold. RMS values are relative errors (j_model − j_data) / (j_data + 3 mA cm⁻²). Where a metric row has an Expected value, it is the published value, or the value used in `model.py`, that the number is compared with.

The 50 mM cathode curve (row 33) is left out of the fit and is the only held-out comparison: the model predicts it with 29 % RMS error. Most of that error comes from a stretch where the published 50 mM curve carries more current than the 100 mM curve, which no model with a positive reaction order can follow; away from it the error is close to that of the fitted curves (details in row 33). No independent full-cell data are available yet to test the absolute cell voltage.
